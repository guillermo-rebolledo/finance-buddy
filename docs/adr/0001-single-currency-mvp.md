# Use Mexican pesos for the first version

Version one records all income and expenses in Mexican pesos (MXN), and its summaries and exports use MXN. Supporting one currency keeps recording and reporting simple, at the cost of postponing financial movements in other currencies. US dollars (USD) and Japanese yen (JPY) are possible future additions; multi-currency reporting and exchange-rate conversion rules remain undecided.
