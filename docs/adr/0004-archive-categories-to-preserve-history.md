# Archive categories to preserve financial history

Manage the separate income and expense category lists on a dedicated Categories page, including adding custom categories and archiving categories that are no longer used. Archiving removes a category from the choices for new financial movements while preserving it on existing entries and in summaries and reports of those entries. This keeps historical categorization intact while allowing the active category lists to stay useful and concise.

The Categories page also supports renaming categories. A rename updates the category label on existing financial movements and newly generated summaries and reports; previously exported snapshots retain the text they contained when generated.

Archived categories can be viewed and restored from the Categories page. Restoring a category makes it available for new financial movements again without changing its existing history.
